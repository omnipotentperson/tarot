What are transits?

In astrology, a natal chart is the sky at birth. Transits are the current sky moving across that natal pattern. A transit is usually read by comparing a current planet to a natal planet or angle, then judging the aspect between them: conjunctions intensify, sextiles open cooperation, squares create friction, trines support flow, and oppositions reveal polarity that asks for integration.

The closer the aspect, the stronger the symbolism. Fast planets describe short moods and timing; slower planets describe longer chapters of pressure, growth, revision, or maturation.
