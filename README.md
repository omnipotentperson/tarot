## 🌒 Overview
**The Veil** is a high-fidelity, single-page web application designed as a digital sanctuary for the modern practitioner. It bridges ancient hermetic wisdom with a clean, "Gold-on-Ink" aesthetic, utilizing hand-drawn iconography to create an immersive experience for self-reflection and occult study.

---

## 🧭 Core Features

### 🎴 Sacred Readings (Draws)
The heart of the application, where users consult the cards for guidance.
* **How to Use:** Navigate to **Readings**, select a spread (e.g., *Celtic Cross*), and click **Draw**.
* **The Magick:** Mimics the physical act of cutting the deck. It uses a randomized engine to pull cards that reflect your current energetic frequency, acting as a mirror for the subconscious.

### 🖐️ The Fan Draw
A tactile, manual interaction for selecting a specific card.
* **How to Use:** Hover over the fanned deck and click the card that "calls" to you.
* **The Magick:** Honors the *Intuitive Pull*. By selecting a card based on feel rather than a button, it facilitates a stronger connection between the user and the digital ether.

### 📖 The Great Archives (Library)
A comprehensive, searchable database of all 78 Tarot cards.
* **How to Use:** Browse and filter by **Major Arcana** or specific **Suits** (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles).
* **The Magick:** Acts as a *Digital Grimoire* for study and meditation, housing the archetypal journey of the Fool.

### 🕯️ Mystical Reference Guide
A modular encyclopedia for ritual correspondences, featuring optimized logic for candles, crystals, and deities.
* **How to Use:** Toggle between **Candles, Crystals, Herbs,** and **Deities** to see magical properties.
* **The Magick:** Ensures the "intent" of a ritual matches the "frequency" of the materials—essential for successful manifestation.
